# Compile a concrete prompt

Resolve every bracketed field. Use this as a construction template, not a fixed theme. Never call an image tool when the current request is skill creation, analysis-only or prompt-only.

“Create one [ratio] archival cyanotype collage on near-white matte paper. [Source-role mapping: image A supplies the subject; image B supplies layout only; etc.] Extract [specific subject and anchors]. Preserve [signature silhouette cues]. Remove [specific irrelevant details]. Reduce subjects to elementary flat shapes using cyanotype polarity. Use one to three broad filled masses per panel and at most one essential negative-space opening. Merge or omit small parts; keep only one source-specific proportion or gesture. Do not produce detailed photograms or tinted photographs.

Use [layout name], with [explicit normalized panel placements] and [module count] visual modules. Panel 01: [content and crop]. Panel 02: [different content]. Panel 03: [different content]. [Additional panels only as planned.] Maintain shared alignments, upright rectangular patches, consistent broad gutters, and about [target] percent genuinely unprinted surrounding paper. Keep one main visual anchor; leave unused space empty.

Within the image patches, use continuous deep Prussian-blue exposure fields and flat paper-white subject shapes. Retain only [one essential silhouette cue]. Use uniform solid fills, no gradients and no descriptive interior lines. Keep paper texture almost invisible and contours clean; no exposure mottling, grain, glow or blurred edges. [Optional single subordinate positive study.] Keep the global page near-white; the instruction to turn non-subject areas blue applies inside image patches.

Set only this exact copy: title [quoted title]; introduction [quoted introduction]; panel captions [quoted numbered captions]. Place text [specific placement]. Use small, readable vintage mechanical typewriter lettering, [Latin monospaced slab serif / Chinese monospaced Ming-like print], subtle ribbon ink variation, left alignment and quiet line spacing. Keep text on clean paper. Preserve the requested language and spelling.

Exclude porcelain or chinoiserie styling, ornate blue floral decoration, ceramic gloss or crackle, blue photo filters, detailed realistic shading, neon glow, generic stock logos, veins, fur, spokes, facial details, tiny windows, foam speckles, hatching, stippling, distressed texture, dense newspaper filler, random rotations, decorative tape, heavy shadows, extra text, fabricated specimen data, and unrelated plants or shells copied from style references.”

## Final prompt check

Verify that all source references exist and their roles are correct; every panel has source-derived content or an explicit theme-derived role; exact copy is supplied; ratios, counts and placements agree; each panel uses one to three large masses with at most one interior opening; fills are flat blue and paper-white; no texture substitutes for design; the page remains predominantly quiet; and the instruction never implies a global blue flood. Delete unused optional template clauses. Do not append the old skill's fixed nine-square grid, mandatory color question, strong halation or two-line-footer-only rule.
